I'm Shim Ahyeong, a student at Sungkyunkwan University majoring in Film, Television, and Multimedia. My passion lies in game design — specifically system design, where I focus on building the underlying rules and mechanics that make a game feel alive.

I'm also deeply interested in level design and content planning — shaping how players experience a world and the stories it tells. I believe the best game design is invisible: the player feels the magic without ever seeing the machinery.

My Little Bookstore — Puzzle Creator

A data-driven puzzle difficulty design tool built for My Little Bookstore, a hexagonal tile-sorting game. The system analyzes 500+ stage configurations, computes integrated difficulty scores using a multi-factor algorithm, and visualizes difficulty distributions across levels — enabling precise, data-backed level design decisions.
